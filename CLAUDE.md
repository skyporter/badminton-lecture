# CLAUDE.md

Static TypeScript web quiz (Vite, Vitest) to learn reading badminton singles play. No server, no backend, no external API, no AI. UI text is in French, with a non-breaking space (U+00A0) before `?`, `!`, `:` and `;`. Code, names and commits are in English. No explanatory comments in code, full explicit names.

## Commands

- `npm test`: run all tests (`vitest run`).
- `npx tsc --noEmit`: type check.
- `npm run build`: `tsc` then `vite build` into `dist/`.
- `npm run dev`: dev server.

## Architecture

- `src/engine/`: pure, deterministic logic. Same situation and same level give the same result, bit for bit. `rules.ts` holds every rule, weight, threshold and colour of the heat map: all values live in `rules.ts` and nowhere else, except the shot catalogue in `shots.ts` (flight times, landing depths, playable zones), which is also part of the rules and must be reviewed together with `rules.ts` when tuning. Other files: `court.ts` (geometry), `shots.ts` (shot catalogue), `generator.ts` (seeded situations), `evaluate.ts` and `analysis.ts` (value map, best return, alternative, score), `recovery.ts`, `status.ts`, `explain.ts` (French explanations), `rng.ts`, `types.ts`.
- `src/ui/`: rendering and input only. `app.ts` (state, `nextState` reducer, wiring), `panel.ts` (top bar controls, shot banner, shot popover, reveal drawer), `legend.ts` (legend and its visibility in `localStorage`), `courtView.ts` (SVG court and players, shared `svgElement` and `SVG_NAMESPACE`), `trajectory.ts` (trajectory code and shared colours), `situationUrl.ts` (`?seed=&level=` read and write), `heatmap.ts` (canvas map), `input.ts` (pointer to court coordinates), `styles.css`.
- `tests/engine/`, `tests/ui/`, `tests/calibration/` (reference situations with expected results; re-run after any change in `rules.ts`).

## Coordinates and players

Court coordinates are in metres: origin top left, x from 0 to 6.10, y from 0 to 13.40. B is at the top (y < 6.70), A at the bottom (y > 6.70), net at y = 6.70. Singles tramlines are at x = 0.46 and x = 5.64. The UI never stores pixels: the cross, the preview and the map are in court coordinates, so they stay aligned after a resize or rotation.

Both players are right-handed (`handedness: 'droitier'` is a parameter, only that value is implemented). A faces -y, so her forehand is at +x. B faces +y, so his forehand is at -x. The forehand side is shown by a white arc around each player disc (`forehandArc` in `courtView.ts`, ±45° around the forehand direction), not by a drawn racket.

## Colours and trajectory code

A `#3b82f6`, B `#e05252`. B's shot is a solid line in B's colour landing on a dot in A's colour; the student's return is dashed in A's colour (cross included); the best return is dashed `#ffffff` (with a white cross at its landing point), unreachable part of the student's return `#ff5252` dotted (when the chosen shot cannot land at the cross depth, the blue trajectory stops at `reachableLanding` from `evaluate.ts` and the drawer line reads « <shot> impossible jusque-là »). Map: bright green `#00e676`, light green `#b2ff59`, red `#ff5252` (constants in `rules.ts`).

Trajectories (top view, `trajectory.ts`, which also holds the player colours): solid for B's shot, `STROKE_PIXELS.returnDashArray` for every return of A; there is no speed code anymore. Only the curvature still varies with the shot (`trajectoryCurvature`: straight = flat, slight curve = low arc, strong curve = high arc). The legend reuses these constants for its swatches and has no trajectory code block.

Stroke widths and dash lengths are constant in screen pixels, whatever the zoom or court size: every stroke in the court SVG carries `vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"` through `constantStroke(...)` and its width comes from `STROKE_PIXELS` (`trajectory.ts`, also used by the legend swatches). Only shapes (discs, landing dot, cross size, forehand arc radius) scale with the court. Each situation writes `?seed=<integer>&level=<level>`, plus `&shot=<shot id>&x=<metres>&y=<metres>` once the answer is validated (the target is rounded to the centimetre in `set-target`, so a reload restores the exact same score; on load and on `popstate` a valid choice restores the reveal through `restore-situation`): « Nouvelle situation » adds a history entry (`history.pushState`), every other change (level, answer) replaces the current one (`history.replaceState`). The same URL on load restores the situation, and browser back/forward (`popstate`, action `restore-situation`) shows the situation of the address bar, as a question or as the reveal when the address carries a choice.

## Layout

Everything happens on the court, on mobile and desktop alike: no side panel. Top to bottom: a thin top bar (`.controls`: level select and « Nouvelle situation »), then the court frame (`.court-frame`, sized from the viewport only) with its overlays. `.shot-banner` (top of the frame) shows `describeShotB` in both phases (« Situation : B fait un amorti rapide en ligne, … »). In the question phase, `.court-hint` (bottom right of the frame, small light text with no background, not clickable) says « Clique sur le terrain de B pour choisir ton retour. ». It never covers the court: the SVG viewBox top margin is `BANNER_RESERVED_METRES` (`courtView.ts`, 0.75 m: exactly the two lines every situation text takes, plus padding) plus 0.4 m of out zone, the banner height is that reservation as a percentage of the viewBox height, and its font is in `cqw` (the frame is a size container), so it keeps the same size in metres at any width; B's back line and 0.4 m of out zone stay visible and clickable. The frame width uses `--court-aspect` (viewBox width / height). A click or tap on the court places the cross and opens `.shot-popover` next to it: the three shot groups (each button has a `title` tooltip from `SHOT_DESCRIPTIONS` in `explain.ts`, and the selected shot's description is also written under the buttons for touch screens), « Valider » (disabled until a shot is chosen) and a close « ✕ ». The popover is positioned in percentages from court coordinates (`--anchor-x`, `--anchor-y`), flips left on the right half (`.to-left`) and up on the lower half (`.upward`), and is clamped inside the frame. Escape (one `document` keydown listener, replaced on each `startApp`) or « ✕ » closes it (the cross stays); clicking the court again moves the cross and reopens it. On touch, dragging moves the preview and the popover opens on release. After validation `.reveal-drawer` overlays the bottom of the frame: collapsed, one line « Score : NN / 100 — Meilleur : <shot> » and a chevron; expanded, the full reveal content (`.choice-box` with a white border: the student's choice, score and its reasons; `.best-box` with a light background, no border and a bright green title: the best return and its reasons; then the « Alternative » line), scrollable, at most 60 % of the court height. It opens expanded right after validation (and when a reload restores a choice); the chevron collapses it. Popover and drawer state live in `QuizState` (`popoverOpen`, `drawerExpanded`, actions `close-popover` and `toggle-drawer`), so `nextState` stays pure; a level change after the reveal keeps the drawer state.

`.trajectory-tags` (HTML, sibling of the SVG, no pointer events) puts the shot name in the middle of each drawn trajectory (`trajectoryMiddle`), positioned in percentages of the viewBox and styled exactly like the « Légende » button: B's shot in the question phase, the student's return once a shot is chosen, and the student's and best returns in the reveal. The bottom right hint is plain white text with no shadow. The small « Légende » button (24 px tall) and the legend box (`.legend-overlay`) sit in `.bottom-stack` with the drawer, so the legend is always above the drawer and never overlaps it. Overlays are siblings of the court SVG, so pointer events on the banner, popover, legend or drawer never reach the court input. Its « Code des trajectoires » block is open by default and keeps the player's choice across re-renders. The legend is visible by default on desktop and hidden on mobile, the choice is remembered in `localStorage` (`badminton-lecture:legend-visible`, every access in `try/catch`).
